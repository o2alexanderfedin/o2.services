Stated weakness

The oracle is blind in one direction. Published π values are quoted at powers of ten, and a power of ten sits far from the prime below it — 999983, 99991, 9973 — so a guest that loses the top of its range returns the right total anyway. That hole is what workload C closes.

The only workload with a real aggregation.
