### The three open phases

**Phase 20 — 6/7. The one with a tractable path, and its ground has shifted.**
Criterion 7: *"A coordinator writes a checkpoint during a live job run through `bin/agent.ts`, and
a SECOND requestor — given nothing but that checkpoint's CID — finishes only the outstanding shards."*
When scored 2026-08-05 the verifier wrote *"No coordinator an operator can run writes a checkpoint."*
**That sentence is now false**: `demo/main.ts:1188` passes `checkpointsInto(node.store)` on no flag,
and `SubmitOptions.checkpoints` became required at `e30090c` so every call site states its intent.
**But `bin/agent.ts` — which the criterion names — has zero checkpoint references**, and the read
half has no supplier anywhere (B4). Not carried to another phase; the verifier requested an owner
ruling it may not take itself. **No verifier has re-read this since the ground moved.**

**Phase 21 — 2/3. Settled; will not close.**

precedent: *"The score does NOT move: criterion 2 stays PARTIAL and Phase 21 stays 2/3."* Cause

predicate over that data can refuse one call and admit the other."* `deferred: []` — carried within
Phase 21, not to any later phase. **15/15 is therefore unreachable by working.**

**Phase 22 — 2/3. Declined on the reading, not the instrument.**

guard decoration."*

**The instrument has a structural blind spot, and the milestone's three worst breaks sit in it.**
The guard walks *callable barrel exports*. `SubmitOptions.resumeFrom`, `BrowserNodeOptions.enrollment`
and `BootstrapInfo.enrollmentProvider` are **option fields with no production supplier** — invisible
to it. B1, B2 and B4 are all of that shape. So the register can be complete and green while the
milestone's purpose is unmet, and the 68 both *overstates* the debt (47 are tracing artefacts) and
*understates* the gap.
