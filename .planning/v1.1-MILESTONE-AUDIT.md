# v1.1 "Wire What Was Built" — Milestone Audit

**Status: `gaps_found`.** One requirement is unsatisfied and two integration blockers stand. The
milestone did most of what it set out to do — and the instrument it built in its final phase is
what makes the residue measurable rather than arguable.

## The headline, stated plainly

This milestone's premise was that v1.0 had built 36 mechanisms nothing called. **Phase 22 built the
guard that finds them, and the guard's first reading is that 67 of 217 callable barrel exports are
still unreachable — 47 with no production caller anywhere.** That is not a failure of the guard; it
is the guard working. The honest summary of v1.1 is: *the wiring that was done is real and is now
verified end to end, and a comparable amount of wiring was not done and is now counted.*

### One premise corrected — and it was mine

I briefed the integration check with STATE.md's account that `bin/seed.ts` has no `--admit-issuer`
and `seed-server.ts` hard-codes `'admits-any-peer'`. **That is stale and I verified the correction
myself**: `--admit-issuer` is at `bin/seed.ts` (repeatable, hex-validated in its own loop),
`SeedServerOptions.relayAdmission` is a **required** field, and `seed-server.ts` passes it straight
through. Landed at `afe8b0b`; criterion 8 re-scored MET at `580e461`.

**So STATE.md now contradicts itself**: Current Position says Phase 24 is closed 1/1, while Session
Continuity still says the seed cannot be told to close *and that this is why criterion 8 does not
close*. The second is false.

**What is genuinely asymmetric now is different and should not be closed casually.** `bin/bench.ts`
hard-codes `relayAdmission: 'admits-any-peer'` at three sites and has no flag mechanism at all.
`relayAdmissionGate`'s own header states the reason to leave it: 24-02's pre-gate baseline *"stays
comparable only while that remains true"*. Arming admission in the bench rig would invalidate the
comparative baseline this milestone's speedup numbers are read against — a deliberate trade with a
stated cost, not an unwired connection.

## 6. What could not be checked, and why

From the integration pass, recorded rather than glossed: no flow was executed at runtime (the pass
was read-only and ran no vitest); `relayAdmissionGate`'s retry budget was read but not measured
against libp2p's 5 s reservation ceiling in the field; the 16 `global-object-hop` dispositions were
confirmed as a class but not individually; and whether `discoverCandidates`' absence from
`bin/agent.ts` is intended is a design question the code shape cannot answer.

## Verdict

**`gaps_found`.** The milestone is not clean and should not be completed as if it were. Two
integration blockers (G1, G2) break real operator flows, one requirement is unsatisfied, and the
guard this milestone built is reporting 47 unwired capabilities.

**None of that is a reason to discard the milestone's work.** Fourteen phases landed with
verifications, the wiring that was done is verified end to end, and v1.1 ends holding something
v1.0 did not: an instrument that makes "built but not wired" a number rather than an argument.
