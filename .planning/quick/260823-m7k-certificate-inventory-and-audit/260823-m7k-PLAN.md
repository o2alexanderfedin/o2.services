# Document every certificate type, and audit the tree against Response 01

## Why this task exists

The owner asked to "review the document describing our certificates — all of their types"
and to "check whether everything is implemented in accordance with the document." Both
halves turned out to be real work rather than a formality:

- The inventory in `RFC-0003-RESPONSE-01-anchoring-and-freshness.md` §0 lists surfaces
  rather than presenting the set as a set, and the set has grown since it was written
  (the X.509 form landed afterwards).
- §0 is itself stale in its last clause — it names `@libp2p/kad-dht` among the things that
  do not exist, and that stopped being true 69 seconds after the commit that wrote it.
- The audit had never been done. Response 01 carries nineteen numbered recommendations
  across §1.8 and §2.10 and nothing in the tree records which of them landed.

## Scope

Docs only. No source changes, no dependency changes.

1. New document `docs/architecture/RFC-0003-RESPONSE-04-certificate-inventory-and-audit.md`:
   the complete inventory (seven artefacts plus an eighth of different nature); why caching
   is safe by construction and where it is not; the audit tables for §2.10 and §1.8; the
   contradiction between two revocation designs; the DHT findings; and a work register.
2. A dated SUPERSEDED note on RESPONSE-01 §0 that preserves the wrong reading.

## Rules this task must not break

- Every claim cites the file and line it was read at, on 2026-08-23. Nothing recalled.
- Rows that were not measured are named as not-measured rather than guessed — the audit
  contains exactly one such row (§4, the second half of recommendation 5).
- Corrections to statements made earlier in the session are recorded, not deleted.
- Commit with explicit paths.
