# v2.0 "Open the Doors" — what shipped, and what it did not

**Closed 2026-09-17 by owner ruling, at 29 of 38 milestone requirements, with the release
`v2.0.0-rc.14` deployed and verified live.**

---

## The honest headline

**The doors are open and nobody has walked through them.**

This milestone's stated goal was two things joined by *and*: make the fabric reachable by real
people on the real internet, **and** take the first genuine scaling curve on hundreds of
independently-owned devices across several continents. The first half is done and running. The
second half did not happen, and closing the milestone does not make it happen.

That is written here in the first paragraph rather than in a footnote, because the milestone's
own `PROJECT.md` entry says why it existed: *"Everything so far was measured on one machine …
Those establish shape and they cannot establish the project's headline claim, which is about
independently-owned nodes."* **The headline claim is still unestablished.** What changed is that
everything needed to establish it now exists and is deployed.

The owner's ruling, 2026-09-17: close v2.0, carry the remainder into the next milestone, and put
the AWS Lambda work first. The nine open rows below are that remainder.

## What this milestone actually built

Twenty-nine requirements closed. The ones that changed what the fabric *is*, rather than what it
contains:

- **A hosted always-on tier on Cloudflare** — a dialable libp2p peer over WSS with a persisted
  identity, a working Circuit Relay v2 server, and a record store with exact expiry. Roles a
  browser mesh structurally cannot supply itself.
- **A kill switch that needs no redeploy**, and reports its own operability rather than being
  assumed to work.
- **Entry conditions for a public cohort** — consent before a CPU cycle, an always-on indicator,
  a stop control, and a six-stage funnel with a frozen telemetry schema.
- **Every stored key is ciphertext** (Phase 43), and nothing states its own operator identity
  any more (Phase 44) — the provider derives it from a key it holds a proof for.
- **`independent` means what it says** (Phase 45). A quorum vouched for by one issuer is
  `single-issuer`. With one provider ruled, `independent` is unreachable — the fabric stopped
  claiming an independence it cannot support.

## What closing this milestone does NOT mean

Stated explicitly, because a shipped milestone is the document people quote later:

1. **The scaling curve was not taken.** BENCH-06 becomes the next milestone's headline
   experiment, exactly as it was this one's.
2. **The fabric has run on one region, not three.** Two of the three named objects do not exist.
3. **No invitation has been sent.** The cohort of a few hundred testers is unspent, which is the
   one resource here that is spendable exactly once.
4. **`independent` is unreachable on this fabric today**, by the owner's ruling of one provider.
   That is correct and it is a ceiling, and the next milestone inherits it.
