## What this milestone learned, in one line each

- **A criterion closes when a verifier says so, not when a file does.** Ten requirement rows still
  read `[ ]` here with their wiring in place, because their remaining clauses are unmet.
- **The reachability guard cannot see demo-page wiring.** 68 unreachable exports before B5's fix
  and 68 after — the tracer cannot cross the `window.o2` hop. Phase 22 is scored on that number.
- **An instrument that refuses your change is the instrument working.** `bin/bench.ts` was named as
  criterion 7's host on a durability premise its own source refutes; its `HarnessIntegrityError`
  caught the shape before the wrong thing shipped.
- **Frontmatter is not a verdict.** Phase 13 has two verification files and the superseded one sorts
  last; phases 16 and 19 closed by dated amendment with frontmatter deliberately untouched.

## Why the source files were NOT deleted

The GSD completion workflow prescribes deleting `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` and collapsing
`ROADMAP.md` to one line. **Both were declined here, measurably:** ten specs read
`REQUIREMENTS.md` — `requirements-ledger`, `acceptance-traceability`, `reachability-guard` and
`commit-scope` among them, all of which gate every commit — and six read `ROADMAP.md`. Deleting
either would redden the guards and leave the repository unable to commit. The archive is a **copy**;
the originals stay live until a v1.2 roadmap replaces them.
