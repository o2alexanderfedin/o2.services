## What this milestone learned, in one line each

- **A criterion closes when a verifier says so, not when a file does.** Ten requirement rows still
  read `[ ]` here with their wiring in place, because their remaining clauses are unmet.
- **The reachability guard cannot see demo-page wiring.** 68 unreachable exports before B5's fix
  and 68 after — the tracer cannot cross the `window.o2` hop. Phase 22 is scored on that number.
- **An instrument that refuses your change is the instrument working.** `bin/bench.ts` was named as
  criterion 7's host on a durability premise its own source refutes; its `HarnessIntegrityError`
  caught the shape before the wrong thing shipped.
- **Frontmatter is not a verdict.** Phase 13 has two verification files and the superseded one sorts
  last; phases 16 and 19 closed by dated amendment with frontmatter deliberately untouched.

## Why the source files were NOT deleted

The GSD completion workflow prescribes deleting `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` and collapsing
`ROADMAP.md` to one line. **Both were declined here, measurably:** ten specs read
`REQUIREMENTS.md` — `requirements-ledger`, `acceptance-traceability`, `reachability-guard` and
`commit-scope` among them, all of which gate every commit — and six read `ROADMAP.md`. Deleting
either would redden the guards and leave the repository unable to commit. The archive is a **copy**;
the originals stay live until a v1.2 roadmap replaces them.

**And one sentence in this file is now false rather than merely stale:**

> **15 of 15 was never reachable.**

It was reachable, and it was reached — within hours of that sentence being written. The reasoning
behind it was sound at the time and is worth keeping for what it shows: it rested on owner ruling
2026-08-05 recording Phase 21 criterion 2 as *a measured negative* whose "score does NOT move".
**What moved was not the score of that clause but the clause's scope** — criterion 2 has a re-tag
half that the ruling never spoke to, and closing it was a different piece of work from the one the
ruling declined. A ruling about one clause of a criterion is not a ruling about the criterion.

**Nothing in the body below is edited.** The five blockers, their commits, the plants and the
lessons are all accurate as written; it is the scoreboard at the top that stopped being true. The
table above is the correction, and the original stays visible so a reader who finds "12 of 15"
quoted elsewhere can see where it came from and why it stood.

**Why this matters beyond bookkeeping.** *An archive is written at the moment a milestone is
declared closed, which is exactly the moment the last work is still landing.* This one was wrong
for two days and nothing noticed, because an archive is the file nobody re-reads. If a future
milestone archives the same way, re-read it once the closing commits have merged.
