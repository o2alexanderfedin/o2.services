## The five blockers, re-verified by symbol rather than by the amendment's line numbers

- **B1 / B2 / B3 — the browser never enrolled.** The four-hop chain is complete:
  `seed.ts` → `seed-server.ts` emits `enrollmentProvider` in `/bootstrap.json` →
  `demo/main.ts` destructures it → `demo/index.html` calls `window.o2.acceptEnrolment()`.
  The last hop, which did not exist, exists.
- **B4 — checkpoint recovery had no production supplier.** `resumeFrom` now has **two**:
  `demo/main.ts` and `bin/agent.ts`. The first pass's *"`bin/agent.ts` has zero checkpoint
  references"* is **false at HEAD** — it imports `checkpointsInto` and `jobIdOf`, reads
  `--resume-from`, recovers, and passes `{checkpoints, resumeFrom}`.
- **B5 — a lifted AOT artifact could not be ingested.** `demo/main.ts` imports and calls
  `fetchModuleForDispatch`, which calls `loadArtifact`.

## Five findings this audit is recording rather than fixing

**1. Six phases read `status: human_needed` and every one is closed on criteria.** The
frontmatter carries the **original** verdict; this project's verifiers *append* amendments
rather than rewrite. Phase 19 nearly cost this audit a false finding: its `score:` field says
`4/5 criteria MET (1 PARTIAL)` and line 1724 of the same file says **`Score 4/5 → 5/5.
Criterion 5 MET`** by dated amendment of 2026-08-07. **Read the amendments, not the header.**

**2. Phase 13's filenames sort against the reader.** `13-VERIFICATION.md` scores 0/3 and is
superseded; `13-VERIFICATION-2.md` scores 3/3 and is live. `sort | tail -1` picks the wrong
one, and a glob for `*VERIFICATION.md` misses the live file entirely. This audit hit both.

**3. The reachability guard has a structural blind spot, and a green register is not evidence
of demo-page wiring.** Option fields (`SubmitOptions.resumeFrom`,
`BrowserNodeOptions.enrollment`, `BootstrapInfo.enrollmentProvider`) and the `window.o2`
object-literal hop are invisible to a declaration-level call graph. B1/B2/B4 are exactly that
shape, which is why they were re-verified here by direct symbol grep. **A future audit must not
substitute the guard for this check.**

**4. Phase 16 is wired more narrowly than its phase claim reads.** `bin/agent.ts` contains no
occurrence of `reduce` or `combine`; tree-reduce reaches production through `bin/bench.ts`
and `demo/main.ts` only. Not a break — the MR-04 row already carries this correction — but
"wired at `bin/agent.ts`" was never true.

**5. Phase 24 has no requirement row of its own.** Its coverage runs through AUTH-01/AUTH-02,
so "50 requirements across phases 11–24" is loose: nothing is assigned to 24 directly.
