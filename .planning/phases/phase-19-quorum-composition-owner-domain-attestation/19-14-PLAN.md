# Phase 19 · Plan 14 — The agreeing set says who signed, not just who answered

**Closes:** the half of the third leg that makes a receipt possible. VER-08/09/10's input.

<objective>
`VerificationResult`'s `agreed` arm carries `agreeing: readonly string[]` — **node-id
strings** (`verify.ts:85-95`). A receipt computed over those is the submitter's word about
itself: the submitter chose the ids, holds the executors, and mints the whole record. That is
the artifact the owner's 2026-08-02 decision exists to replace.

Plan 19-13 put a signature on every ok `ExecutionOutcome` and carried it onto `runOne`'s
internal `Receipt`. **Nothing can read it yet**, because `executeVerified` discards the
receipts and returns names. This plan exposes it.

**The shape is one field, not two.** The obvious cheaper change — keep `agreeing` as strings
and add a sibling `attestations` array — is refused for the reason `19-CONTEXT.md` already
gives against a parallel `nodeId → certificate` map: *"a second source of truth that can
disagree, with nothing able to catch the disagreement."* Here the disagreement would be
positional and silent. Worse, it would leave a downstream reader a **choice** between building
a receipt from names and building one from signatures, and the whole point of this leg is that
there is no such choice. Making `agreeing` itself carry the attestation removes it.

The cost is honest and counted: about **30 read sites across 11 files**, measured 2026-08-02.
Every one becomes a projection — the same node ids, one `.map` away. That is the price of
adding a signature to what a result *is*, and it is paid here rather than hidden behind an
optional field.

Output: an agreed arm a receipt can be derived from without trusting the requestor.
</objective>

## Recorded decisions

- **One field, not a sibling array.** A positional correspondence between names and signatures
  is a second source of truth that can disagree silently, and it would leave a downstream
  reader a choice between building a receipt from names and from signatures. This leg exists to
  remove that choice.
- **No flattening helper.** A convenience returning the old `readonly string[]` is a
  convenience a receipt would eventually be built from.
- **`tab-api.ts` is not edited.** The reported type is already node ids and already correct;
  editing it would collide with 19-04 and 19-11 for no gain.
- **The compared digest is untouched.** VER-05 covers `(task, output)`; an attestation is
  per-node and travels beside it.
- **Only the `agreed` arm changes.** Attestations on a disagreement would invite picking a
  winner, which `verify.ts`'s header forbids.

## Out of scope

- Composing the signing wrapper. Plan 19-15 — until it lands, every real replica reports the
  sentinel, and that is expected.
- Building the receipt. Plan 19-06 consumes this field.
- Any display change. The demo reports the same ids; the receipt's display is 19-10 and 19-11.
- `packages/browser/src/tab-api.ts`. Not touched here.
