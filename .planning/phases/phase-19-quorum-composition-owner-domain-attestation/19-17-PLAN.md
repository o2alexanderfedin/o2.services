# Phase 19 · Plan 17 — Two receipts, because there are two claims

**Closes:** the reading half of the combine signing leg. VER-08/09/10 for a reduced job.

<objective>
Plan 19-16 made every combining node sign what it merged and what it produced, and carried
those statements out on `ReduceOutcome`. **Nothing checks them and nothing displays them**,
which is the condition this whole milestone exists to remove, arriving in a new place. This
plan closes it.

**Two receipts, and conflating them would be worse than having one.** `PROJECT.md` splits the
verification claim and the split is not a formality:

| claim | what carries it | where it is reported |
|---|---|---|
| this shard's **result** was computed by certified nodes that agreed | `submitJob`'s per-shard and per-job receipt | `ShardResult` / `JobResult` (Plan 19-06) |
| the **aggregation** over those shards was performed by certified nodes | `reduceJob`'s aggregate receipt | `ReduceJobResult` (this plan) |

A sovereign map is `owner-attested` by construction — pinning data to one owner removes the
second executor. The aggregation *over* those contributions can be redundant, because a combine
reads only content-addressed partials and is runnable anywhere. `reduce.ts:280-287` states this
in the source already. **So the two receipts routinely differ, and a reduced job that reported
only one of them would be making the stronger-sounding claim about the weaker half.**

Output: a reduction that says how strongly its own aggregation is attested, printed beside the
map receipt rather than instead of it.
</objective>

## Recorded decisions

- **Two receipts, never one.** The map half and the aggregation half are different claims and
  routinely differ, because a sovereign map is owner-attested by construction while the
  aggregation over it can be redundant. Printing one for both would make the stronger-sounding
  claim about the weaker half.
- **`reduceJob` takes a required issuer set; `submitJob` does not.** `submitJob` reaches its
  trust decision through the certificate discovery already verified at the seam;
  `reduceJob`'s executors are bare peer ids with no certificate anywhere on the path. The
  asymmetry is structural and is written down at both sites.
- **The dedupe-by-node-key invariant lives at the caller, not in `attestationReceipt`.** That
  function is a pure report over whatever it is handed; a dedupe inside it would hide an
  assembly bug instead of failing on one. Plan 19-06 holds the same invariant for the map half.
- **The weakest combine sets the aggregate strength.** A tree makes it easy for one weak step to
  disappear under a strong root.
- **A run with no reduce prints no aggregate receipt.** *No aggregation happened* and *the
  aggregation was not attested* are different statements.
- **A signed aggregation is not a correct one.** Reduce redundancy and `disagreements` stay
  exactly as they are.
