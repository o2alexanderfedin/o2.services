# Phase 19 · Plan 15 — Wired: both factories sign, and a stranger can check it

**Closes:** the third leg. The precondition for `attestationReceipt` being an attestation
rather than a self-report.

<objective>
Plan 19-13 built the signature and Plan 19-14 carried it to a reader. **Neither composed it
anywhere**, so today every replica in the fabric reports the unsigned sentinel — truthfully,
and uselessly. This plan is the composition site, and it is the plan that decides whether the
third leg is *built* or *wired*.

**That distinction has a guard in this repository already, and it is leg 1's.**
`packages/node/src/trust-anchors.node.test.ts:366-394` asserts **textually** that each listed
construction site composes `guardModuleProvenance(` — the code-signing leg. Its own comment at
`:653` records why: composing the wrapper *at the construction site* is the fix, and a wrapper
that exists but is not composed measures nothing. Leg 3 gets the same treatment in the same
file, so the two legs are proven wired the same way and neither can quietly stop being.

**Then the claim is measured across a real process boundary, because that is the only place it
means anything.** The owner's statement of what the leg buys is precise:

> a third party holding **only the provider's public key** can verify the result came from a
> node that provider enrolled, running code that provider published.

An in-process spec cannot show that — the requestor built the executor. This plan spawns real
`bin/agent.ts` processes, runs a shard across them, and hands the resulting attestations to a
verifier constructed **with the provider's public key and nothing else**: no descriptor, no
peer id, no connection. That verifier either checks out or it does not, and either way it is
the reading VER-08/09/10 rest on.

Output: a fabric whose results are signed, and a measurement that says what a stranger can
learn from one.
</objective>

## Recorded decisions

- **Outermost.** The wrapper signs the outcome that leaves the node, so nothing composed later
  can alter a signed answer.
- **Unconditional.** A node with no certificate composes the same wrapper and writes the
  no-signer literal. A branch is a thing a later reader extends; a literal is a thing they have
  to write.
- **The signer is not configurable.** A node signing with anything but the key its certificate
  names produces attestations that will not verify, and there is no reason to make that
  expressible.
- **The two bench rigs are not edited.** Neither holds a certificate; their executors report
  the sentinel, which is truthful. Recorded so it is not read as an omission.
- **The guard is textual and comment-stripped**, exactly as leg 1's is. Both legs are now
  proven composed the same way.
- **No timing claim in this file.** Its claim is about what verifies.
- **A signature is not correctness.** `executeVerified` is still the only thing that says an
  answer is right, and both compositions say so at the line.

## Out of scope

- Building the receipt from these attestations. Plan 19-06, wave 3 alongside this one — it
  works from unit-level signed outcomes and does not depend on this plan's composition.
- The issuance budget's durable ledger. Plan 19-07.
- Signing a **combine** result. Deferred in `19-CONTEXT.md`; the leg covers `exec` only.
- Any certificate-lifetime or renewal change. Owner correction 2026-08-02: the attack radius
  does not justify it, and results being signed and attributable is one of the four reasons
  why — which this plan is what makes true.
- Any measurement of signing cost. Its own task, on a quiet host.
