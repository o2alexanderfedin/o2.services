# Phase 19 · Plan 11 — The label a visitor reads

**Closes:** criterion 3's demo-UI half, and VER-10 on the surface with the widest audience.

<objective>
Criterion 3 asks that the receipt read owner-attested rather than verified **wherever it is
displayed**, and it names the demo UI. A receipt nothing renders is *built, not wired* — the
condition this milestone exists to eliminate, reappearing in a new place.

**And the page currently asserts something stronger than it knows.** `demo/index.html:369-373`
prints, unconditionally:

> *"Verification cost N× — each cube ran twice, on different nodes, and the two had to agree."*

That sentence is composed by the page, not by the kernel, and it is true only when the run
really did place two replicas on two different nodes. At redundancy 1 it is simply false. And
even at redundancy 2 *"on different nodes"* says nothing about whether those nodes were two
operators, one owner's two machines, or — with a peer list the visitor could not verify —
something else. **That is the conflation VER-10 forbids, already shipped, on the surface with
the widest audience.** This plan replaces the page's claim with the kernel's.

**Where the certificates come from.** A tab holds its own, since Phase 17 gave browser peers
their own persisted identity and enrolment (`BrowserNode.certificate: NodeCertificate | null`,
`browser-node.ts:521`). A peer's is fetchable over the `records` request the browser tier
already **serves** on identical terms to the Node tier (`browser-node.ts:1164`, byte-identical
to `fabric-node.ts:1566`) — so reading one here is the other half of NET-06's own claim that a
browser peer participates in routing as a full peer, exercised from the reading side.
</objective>

## Recorded decisions

- **The visitor-facing report changes, and the exception is named.** `TabColouringRun` has
  been deliberately held stable; the criterion names this surface, so it moves, and the doc
  says why rather than leaving the reader to notice.
- **A peer the tab cannot name is still a peer that computes.** Excluding it would be a node
  class created by a display concern, which is the one thing a decision here is forbidden to
  introduce.
- **The page renders `describeAttestation`'s sentence, never its own.** One source of the
  words is the only way the CLI and the UI cannot describe one result differently.
- **The old unconditional redundancy sentence was a shipped overstatement**, not a wording
  choice. It is corrected as a defect.

## Out of scope

- Discovery. The peers are the ones already connected; nothing here changes who is asked to
  compute.
- The CLI. Plan 19-10.
- Anything about quorum composition. The demo runs public jobs and the composition rules apply
  to it exactly as they do to any other caller; this plan displays what came back.
