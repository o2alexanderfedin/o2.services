# Phase 19 · Plan 12 — The ledger, and what may honestly be ticked

**Closes:** nothing new. Records what the other sixteen plans established, and refuses to
record more than that.

<objective>
Two failure modes this repository has already paid for, both of which this plan exists to
avoid repeating.

**Ledger drift.** Phase 18 found **23 of ~45 ledger citations had outrun the tree, nine of
them introduced by the very plan written to correct drift**, and lost entries M14 and M8
outright — M14 to a renamed test title and M8 to a changed source line. The cheap guard checks
that `find` still matches; it does **not** check that `signature` still does, which is how M2b
sat green for a phase saying *"four sentinels"* against a test saying *"three"*.

**Ledger overstatement.** A requirement can leave *Built, not wired* without arriving at
*done*. AUTH-03 did exactly that in Phase 15. And the guard that was supposed to catch stale
rows read **only** the *Built, not wired* population until 2026-08-02, so **the act of fixing
a row was the act of exempting it** — three rows went stale that way and a fourth, NET-06, had
never been reported at all. `requirements-ledger.node.test.ts` now reads **every** row, so
anything written here is checked.

This plan writes down what happened, at the precision it happened, and no further.
</objective>

## Recorded decisions

- **An unobserved signature is not written.** A ledger entry that was never seen fail belongs
  to the same family as the tautology Phase 18 shipped, and the ledger's own doc already says a
  signature is output rather than a prediction.
- **Rows move only as far as what landed supports.** A requirement can leave *Built, not wired*
  without arriving at done, and the ledger has to be able to say so — otherwise the only way to
  record progress is to overstate it.
- **VER-08/09/10 cannot be ticked on a receipt built from a lookup, nor on half the claim.**
  The owner's 2026-08-02 decisions make the checked signature the evidence, for the aggregation
  as much as for the map. A receipt over node ids is the thing being replaced, not a weaker
  version of the thing being delivered.
- **AUTH-04's row names the enrolment DoS the budget opens.** It was accepted as a trade, and a
  trade recorded as a defence is how the next reader stops looking.
- **AUTH-04's row states the mechanism honestly or stays open.** A bound made durable is not a
  per-identity price, and the row says which one landed.
- **The criterion text is not amended here.** Plan 19-03 made this phase's only sanctioned
  corrections, all of them factual statements about the source rather than adjustments to what
  the phase must achieve.
