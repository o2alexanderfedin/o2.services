# Phase 19 · Plan 06 — The quorum on the path, and the receipt on the result

**Closes:** the kernel half of criteria 1, 2 and 3, and the production-caller half of VER-03,
VER-04, VER-08, VER-09 and VER-10.

<objective>
`composeQuorum`, `attestationReceipt` and `resolveReplicaSets` have **zero production
callers** — barrel re-exports and their own specs, nothing else. Plan 19-01 put the
certificate on the descriptor `submitJob` already carries, Plan 19-02 gave `composeQuorum` the
anchor rule VER-03 never had and the strength it always declared, and Plans 19-13/19-14 made
each agreeing replica carry **what it signed**. This plan is the call site.

**The receipt is derived from signatures, and that is what this plan is for.** Owner decision
2026-08-02: agreement attested by transport authentication only is not transferable, so a
receipt over node-id strings is *the submitter's word about itself*. `agreeing` now carries a
`ResultAttestation` per replica — a certificate plus a signature over **this task and this
output**. A certificate counts toward the receipt only when that signature checks out. What
`attestationReceipt` receives is therefore *"certificates whose holders signed this result"*,
not *"certificates the requestor looked up for the ids it chose"*, and those are different
claims even when they contain the same bytes.

**Two paths, and confusing them would break both.** `PROJECT.md` splits the verification
claim, and the split falls out of the code exactly:

| Data | Mechanism | Reachable strength |
|---|---|---|
| public / shared | `composeQuorum` — anti-affinity, one node per operator, one anchor | `independent` |
| sovereign (owner-pinned) | the owner's own nodes; the aggregation is what gets verified | `owner-domain`, or `owner-attested` at one node |

`composeQuorum` holds **one certificate per operator by construction** (`quorum.ts:123-126`),
so it can never return `owner-domain` and would refuse a sovereign shard with
`insufficient-operators` — correctly, because a sovereign shard on one owner's nodes has one
operator and that is the whole point. **A sovereign shard must therefore never be handed to
it.** The label is what carries the difference, and it is already on the shard.

**Criteria 2 and 3 are one expression on different inputs**, which is what makes the pair worth
trusting: one verified replica is `owner-attested`, two verified replicas sharing an
`operatorId` are `owner-domain`, two operators are `independent`. Nothing special-cases either.

Output: a `JobResult` whose every shard says how strongly it was attested — or says that no
signed statement about it is held — and a quorum refusal that reaches the caller instead of
being silently absorbed into a placement.
</objective>

## Recorded decisions

- **A certificate counts only when its holder's signature over this result checks out.** The
  owner's 2026-08-02 decision, applied at the one place it changes an artifact: a receipt over
  unsigned results is the weaker thing the third leg exists to replace.
- **The trust anchor is the descriptor's certificate, not a new `submitJob` parameter.** The
  pinned-issuer decision was already made at the discovery seam; a second copy of it could
  disagree with the first with nothing able to catch it, and threading one through would touch
  four production submitters.
- **The attestation's certificate must be the descriptor's.** Otherwise a node answers under a
  different certificate of its own and the receipt counts an operator nobody placed with.
- **The receipt reads the agreeing set, never the placed set.** A node that was placed and then
  failed attested nothing.
- **A partial verification is a named absence, not a weaker strength.** `owner-attested` over
  one verified replica of three sounds stronger than the truth, which is that the requestor
  cannot account for two of them.
- **Sovereign shards never reach `composeQuorum`.** One owner is one operator; the composer
  would refuse them correctly and uselessly. The split is `PROJECT.md`'s and is now expressed
  in a branch rather than in prose.
- **A quorum is composed only at redundancy ≥ 2.** Where there is no verification there is no
  quorum, and VER-03 anchors quorums.
