# Phase 19 · Plan 06 — The quorum on the path, and the receipt on the result

**Closes:** the kernel half of criteria 1, 2 and 3, and the production-caller half of VER-03,
VER-04, VER-08, VER-09 and VER-10.

<objective>
`composeQuorum`, `attestationReceipt` and `resolveReplicaSets` have **zero production
callers** — barrel re-exports and their own specs, nothing else. Plan 19-01 put the
certificate on the descriptor `submitJob` already carries, and Plan 19-02 gave
`composeQuorum` the anchor rule VER-03 never had and the strength it always declared. This
plan is the call site.

**Two paths, and confusing them would break both.** `PROJECT.md` splits the verification
claim, and the split falls out of the code exactly:

| Data | Mechanism | Reachable strength |
|---|---|---|
| public / shared | `composeQuorum` — anti-affinity, one node per operator, one anchor | `independent` |
| sovereign (owner-pinned) | the owner's own nodes; the aggregation is what gets verified | `owner-domain`, or `owner-attested` at one node |

`composeQuorum` holds **one certificate per operator by construction** (`quorum.ts:123-126`),
so it can never return `owner-domain` and would refuse a sovereign shard with
`insufficient-operators` — correctly, because a sovereign shard on one owner's nodes has one
operator and that is the whole point. **A sovereign shard must therefore never be handed to
it.** The label is what carries the difference, and it is already on the shard.

**The receipt needs no quorum at all.** `VerificationResult.agreed` carries
`agreeing: readonly string[]` — node ids, which map to descriptors, which now carry
certificates. `classifyAttestation` over those certificates answers all three criteria
without a special case: one agreeing node is `owner-attested`, two of one owner's nodes are
`owner-domain`, two operators are `independent`. Criteria 2 and 3 are the same expression
evaluated on different inputs, which is what makes them worth trusting.

Output: a `JobResult` whose every shard says how strongly it was attested, and a quorum
refusal that reaches the caller instead of being silently absorbed into a placement.
</objective>

      through `publicNodes` will get, and it is reported rather than defaulted.

      — carries the named absence as well, with the reason. A receipt computed over the
      subset that happened to have certificates would overstate or understate, and which of
      the two would depend on an accident.

      `attestationRank`, or the named absence if any shard carries one. A job is no stronger
      than its weakest shard, and `attestationRank` exists so that comparison does not depend
      on remembering the order of a string union.

      figures and `complete` are untouched.

    - A quorum is composed for a shard **only** when all three hold: the shard is
      `label: 'public'`; `spec.redundancy >= 2`; and every candidate carries a certificate.
      Each condition is stated with its reason at the branch, because each will look like an
      escape hatch to somebody who does not know why it is there.
      - **Public only** — a sovereign shard has one owner and therefore one operator, so
        `composeQuorum` would refuse it with `insufficient-operators`, correctly and
        uselessly. Sovereign agreement is the replica-set path (Plan 19-09).
      - **Redundancy ≥ 2** — at redundancy 1 there is no verification (VER-06 makes it a dial
        reaching 1, off) and therefore no quorum. VER-03 requires an anchor in *every
        verification quorum*; where there is no quorum there is nothing to anchor.
      - **All candidates certificated** — a requestor holding no certificates cannot compose
        anything, and refusing its job would break every caller that builds descriptors
        through `publicNodes`. **This is the condition that most needs its argument written
        down**: it is not a silent degradation, because the job's own receipt reports the
        named absence on every shard, so a caller that supplies no certificates gets a result
        that says no attestation was established. `submitJob` runs in the requestor's own
        process and a requestor that supplies nothing bounds only its own claim — the same
        argument `JobSpec.admit`'s doc already makes for itself, and the same shape.
    - When a quorum is composed, its refusal is a **shard-level outcome the caller sees**:
      the shard comes back `unplaceable`-equivalent with the composer's own `reason`, in the
      composer's words. *"Refused rather than silently accepted"* is criterion 1's own
      wording and the refusal has to arrive somewhere a caller reads.
    - When a quorum composes, its `members` are the pool handed to placement for that shard.
      Placement still runs, `eligibleNodes` still gates, and the standing rule holds: the
      quorum does not re-derive who *could* run the shard, it narrows an already-eligible set
      by a constraint placement has no way to express. **A hard constraint applied after a
      load preference is not a constraint** — this repository has recorded that shape twice,
      as NET-08's cap after the loop and as 16-06's bound below the fetch.
    - **The cost of that narrowing is recorded, not hidden.** With the pool equal to the
      quorum size, a shard on the offer arm has nothing left to re-pick onto if a member
      refuses; such a shard comes back unplaceable naming the refusal. That is a worse
      liveness answer than before and a strictly better correctness answer, and the trade is
      written at the branch. If a later phase wants both, the mechanism is a larger quorum
      with a chosen subset, which is a new decision and not a widening of this one.
    - `attestationReceipt` still reads the agreeing set. A composed quorum does not
      pre-declare the strength — the members are who was *asked*, and the receipt reports who
      *agreed*.
  </behavior>

## Recorded decisions

- **The receipt reads the agreeing set, never the placed set.** A node that was placed and
  then failed attested nothing.
- **A job with no certificates in hand reports a named absence, not a strength.** Two
  replicas agreed and the requestor cannot say whose — a third statement, distinct from both
  `owner-attested` and `independent`, and the only honest one.
- **Sovereign shards never reach `composeQuorum`.** One owner is one operator; the composer
  would refuse them correctly and uselessly. The split is `PROJECT.md`'s and is now expressed
  in a branch rather than in prose.
- **A quorum is composed only at redundancy ≥ 2.** Where there is no verification there is no
  quorum, and VER-03 anchors quorums.
- **Narrowing the pool to the quorum costs the offer arm its re-pick on that shard**, and the
  trade is written at the branch rather than discovered later.

## Out of scope

- `resolveReplicaSets` on this path. The receipt needs only the agreeing certificates; the
  replica set is a *discovery* answer and its production caller is Plan 19-01's
  `CandidateSet.replicaSets`. Plan 19-09 measures it end to end.
- Any display of the receipt. CLI is 19-10, demo UI is 19-11.
- The re-pick. `executeVerified` is still called once per shard; WIRE-04 is Phase 20
  criterion 1 and mutation-ledger entry M36 already fires when it lands. **Do not add a
  retry here** — it would spring a tripwire another phase is holding.
