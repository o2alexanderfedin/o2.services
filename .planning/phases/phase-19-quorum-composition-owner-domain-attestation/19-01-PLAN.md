# Phase 19 · Plan 01 — The certificate discovery already held

**Closes:** the type gap that makes every other plan in this phase impossible, and AUTH-05's
producer half.

<objective>
`composeQuorum`, `attestationReceipt` and `resolveReplicaSets` all take
`readonly NodeCertificate[]`. `submitJob` carries `readonly NodeDescriptor[]`
(`packages/core/src/sovereignty.ts:39-55`) — `{nodeId, ownerId, canExecuteSovereign, load}`,
with no certificate, no operator, no relays and no discoverability. **None of the three can
be called anywhere on the path a job actually runs**, and that — not a missing call site —
is why all three have been *Built, not wired* since Phase 6.

The certificate is not missing. It is **discarded**, at one place:
`packages/net/src/discover-candidates.ts:161-186` loops `DiscoveredExecutor`
(`{nodeKey, certificate, capabilities}`) and keeps `certificate.userKey` alone. Every fact
the quorum machinery needs — `operatorId`, `discoverability`, `relayIds`, `userKey` — is in
scope on that line and thrown away three lines later.

This plan stops discarding it. One seam, no `JobSpec` change.

**Why not thread certificates through `JobSpec`.** Four production submitters would each
have to supply them (`bin/bench.ts:759`, `packages/bench/src/perf-workload.ts:345`,
`packages/browser/demo/main.ts:449` and `:682`), and a caller that forgot would get silent
degradation — the exact shape WIRE-01 exists to prevent.

**Why not a parallel `nodeId → certificate` map.** A second source of truth that can
disagree with `NodeDescriptor`, with nothing able to catch the disagreement.

Output: a descriptor that either carries its certificate or states that it has none, and a
`CandidateSet` that reports the replica sets discovery already verified.
</objective>

  readonly userKey: PublicKeyHex
  readonly certificates: readonly NodeCertificate[]

## Recorded decisions

- **Required with a named absence, not optional.** An optional field would let a descriptor
  reach the quorum step carrying nothing, indistinguishable from a builder that forgot. The
  repository has shipped that shape twice and written it down both times.
- **The certificate rides on the descriptor, not beside it.** A parallel `nodeId →
  certificate` map is a second source of truth that can disagree with the first, and
  nothing could catch the disagreement.
- **`replicaSets` is computed where the certificates were verified.** Recomputing the
  grouping at a call site would re-verify against a clock that has moved.

## Out of scope

- Any reader of the new field. `composeQuorum`'s anchor rule is 19-02; `submitJob`'s use of
  the certificate is 19-06. This plan carries the fact; it changes no decision.
- The `--owner-id` / `userKey` unification. Named here, closed in 19-09.
- `JobSpec`. Unchanged, deliberately — see the objective.
