# Phase 19 · Plan 18 — Every submitter says what it wants when verification is not available

**Closes:** nothing behavioural. Makes Plan 19-06's ruling expressible, and makes not choosing
impossible.

<objective>
**Owner ruling 2026-08-03, recorded in `19-CONTEXT.md` under *"The quorum is the default and it
is OPTIONAL"*.** A public shard at `redundancy >= 2` whose candidate set cannot compose a valid
quorum **degrades by default** — it runs at whatever redundancy is available, is marked
degraded, and its receipt reports the weaker strength. It does **not** fail the job. The
exception is caller-set: a caller who genuinely requires independent verification, and would
rather have nothing than a weaker answer, asks for refusal explicitly.

**This plan adds the dial and nothing else.** No behaviour changes, because nothing reads it
until Plan 19-06 in the next wave. That is deliberate and it is this phase's established shape:
Plans 19-13 and 19-14 each landed a type and its fan-out one wave ahead of the code that
consumed it, and both stayed green because a plan said when the arrival was scheduled.

**Why it is required rather than optional, and why that is not negotiable.** This phase has
measured the failure **twice**, on its own plants: Plan 19-01 and Plan 19-13 each planted *"make
it optional and omit it"* and each observed **`tsc --noEmit` exit 0 while the behavioural
assertion failed**. An optional strictness field would let every existing call site silently
mean *degrade* without ever having said so, which is the precise defect this milestone exists to
remove. Every current caller states its choice. **The fan-out is the point, not the cost.**

Output: a `JobSpec` on which not choosing is not expressible, and about thirty-odd call sites
that now say what they want.
</objective>
