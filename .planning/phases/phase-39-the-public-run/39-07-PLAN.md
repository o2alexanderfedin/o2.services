`vitest.config.ts` § *"So this is the procedure, and it is not optional"* (around line 792) has five steps.
**Steps 4 and 5 are what a file-count reconciliation owes; steps 1 to 3 are what a span-table retake owes.**

- **Step 4 — re-derive `NODE_MEASUREMENT.files` independently, at least twice, ideally by three routes that
  share no code, and DIFF THE LISTS, DO NOT COMPARE THE COUNTS.** *"Three equal counts over three different
  sets agree numerically and are wrong."* And: *"Re-derive; do not adjust. The 2026-08-24 pass wrote
  `204 -> 203` by subtracting a file it had watched leave, which is a defensible-looking move that was wrong
  by five, because five had arrived while it was not counting."*
- **Step 5 — record how many files were compared and how many disagreed** in
  `NODE_MEASUREMENT.crossCheckedFiles` / `crossCheckDisagreed`.
- **Step 1 — record the host conditions**, including when nothing had to be waited for: *"the host was
  checked and was quiet is a measurement, and the run that skipped taking it is the run that was discarded at
  ratio 0.48."*

`NODE_MEASUREMENT` fields this plan moves: `files` (236), `unitFiles` (158), `tests` (2948), `unitTests`
(2317), and one `MEASURED_NODE_SPANS` row per added file.

The identity `slow-specs.node.test.ts` asserts:
`measurementField('unitFiles') === measurementField('files') - excludedInNode.length`.

**The precedent for an incremental addition**, from `NODE_MEASUREMENT.files`'s own docblock:
*"**235 -> 236 on 2026-09-02 (Phase 36, RUN-02)**, one more:
`packages/node/src/kill-switch-propagation.e2e.test.ts` (**e2e** lane …). Measured
`real 41.76 / 42.55 / 41.65` s across three runs on a quiet host … `unitFiles` moves by the same one."*
Follow that shape: name each file, its lane, its measured `real` across three runs, and one sentence on why
it costs what it costs.

**The guard's own globs are a reimplementation** and have silently fallen open once
(`tools/**` moved projects and this file kept walking `tools/`). So one of the two derivation routes must
**not** be the guard's own walk.
