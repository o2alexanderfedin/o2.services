## Phase Goal

**As a** project owner, **I want to** send the first invite only after seven conditions hold on a dated
checklist with named evidence for each, and to send invites in stages by region and by cohort slice with
the funnel read between them, **so that** the fabric's headline claim is finally measured on hundreds of
independently-owned devices rather than asserted.

and it says so at the top rather than in a footnote.

mid-run exercise. Criterion 4's distinct-machine half **cannot be discharged here at all unless the owner's
row-8 ruling was `announce-coarse` and the announcement shipped in the release** — otherwise it stays
**descoped and unmeasured — not met**, exactly as `BENCH-06`'s own row says, and a same-host figure may not

Purpose: produce the readings only a real public run can produce, and record what was and was not measured.
Output: `39-RUN-RECORD.md`.
</objective>

    Work `39-KILL-SWITCH-DURING-RUN.md` § 3, at a stage boundary, after that stage's funnel reading and
    before the next invite. Its six steps, in order:
    1. Start the sampler with the exact command line in that document.
    2. Open one of your own tabs on the published page and let it take work.
    3. Write the halt. **Production has one region today**, so this halt is cohort-global — that is a fact
       about the deployment, not a defect, and Phase 33 is its blocker.
    4. Read back three things: `admission.halted` `true` and `admission.since` set on `/self`; the status page
       reading `NOT ADMITTING NEW TASKS`; your own tab's title carrying `■ Not taking new work — ` within one
       poll interval. Note that `inFlight > 0` outranks `halted` in the title, so a tab still draining
       correctly shows computing.
    5. **Un-halt, and read all three back returning.** A control exercised and left on is an outage.
    6. Record the sampler's final line: the observed window, the ratio and the baseline verdict.

    **What means stop:** no observed transition inside three poll intervals; the status page and `/self`
    disagreeing; or a keyless write succeeding.

    **What a window outside the band means.** It is a **finding**, not a failed exercise — criterion 5's
    question is whether a control that works at three tabs works at three hundred, and a number outside
    29 880 ± 1 500 ms is the answer to that question rather than a broken measurement. Record it as measured.
  </how-to-verify>
