## Phase Goal

**As a** project owner, **I want to** send the first invite only after seven conditions hold on a dated
checklist with named evidence for each, and to send invites in stages by region and by cohort slice with
the funnel read between them, **so that** the fabric's headline claim is finally measured on hundreds of
independently-owned devices rather than asserted.

<objective>

ticked box in `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` as of 2026-09-04, and no dated checklist naming their evidence
exists anywhere in the tree.

weak version of this criterion: this repository already has the shape of the strong version in
`acceptance-traceability.node.test.ts` and `requirements-ledger.node.test.ts`, both of which read a document
and fail on a claim that stopped being true.

Purpose: RUN-01 is a gate over other phases' outputs, and *"a row with no named evidence is a no-go, not a
judgement call."* The document is what makes that sentence operable by somebody other than its author.
Output: `39-GO-NO-GO.md` and `go-no-go-checklist.node.test.ts`.
</objective>
