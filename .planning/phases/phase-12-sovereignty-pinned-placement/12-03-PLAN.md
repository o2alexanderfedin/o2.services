<objective>
Prove ROADMAP criterion 1 literally: "A job submitted through `bin/agent.ts`... places
its map task only on that owner's nodes; a test that applies artificial load pressure
specifically to force relocation onto a non-owner node fails to move it." Plan 12-02's
proof runs entirely inside one Vitest process against in-memory executors — sufficient
to prove the wiring, but not sufficient to prove it survives a real OS-process boundary,
which is exactly the gap `two-process.node.test.ts` (Phase 2/NET-01) exists to close for
the non-sovereignty case. This plan is that same closing move for sovereignty.

No new mechanism is built here — `submitJob`'s placement engine (Plan 12-01) and its
discrimination proof (Plan 12-02) already exist and are already mutation-tested. This
plan's only job is to show the mechanism holds when the "owner's node" and the "foreign
nodes" are genuinely separate processes sharing nothing but a socket, dispatched to via
`bin/agent.ts` exactly as `two-process.node.test.ts` already proves for the
non-sovereignty case. No new mutation test is required here — the mutation that matters
(submit.ts's placement-request construction) was already planted and observed to fail in
Plan 12-02; re-planting it against three real child processes would prove the same fact
more slowly for no new evidence.
</objective>
