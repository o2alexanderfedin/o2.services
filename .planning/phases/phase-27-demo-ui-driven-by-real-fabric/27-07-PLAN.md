<objective>
Wire bring-your-own — UI-SPEC section 4.5's thirteen regions — over `TabApi.runJob`, and
give the page the caller **audit finding G4 says it does not have**. This is the half of
G4 the roadmap names; the other half is the primes entry point, which Plan 27-06 left
open with its reason.

The design question this surface carries is the reason the roadmap put it late:
**a page that accepts a module CID must also accept a signed record.** `runJob` requires
`moduleRecord` by construction, and its own docblock says why — a tab started through
`start` always pins some anchor set, so a dispatch with no record is a dispatch every
executor refuses, and *making the field optional would let a caller write the refusal
rather than the job and discover it as a timeout*. A CID-only form would produce nothing
but refusals discovered as timeouts. So: seven inputs, all required, and the copy above
them says the true thing before anything is submitted rather than after.

This is also the one surface in the demo where the egress panel's **refusal arm can go
live**. Every cube `runColouring` submits is `label: 'public'`, so `violations` is empty
by construction everywhere else. `runJob` with `sovereign` set registers owner rows, and
`egressLines`'s withheld branch — written in 2026-08-08 against a path with no caller —
becomes reachable here with no change to that function. That was said at the time and it
is being cashed now.

Purpose: close G4's `runJob` half, and make the provenance check readable rather than
discoverable.
Output: `surfaces/byo.ts`, the form and thirteen regions, `WIRED_SURFACES` gaining
`'byo'`, and `demo-byo.e2e.test.ts` covering the accepted and the refused arm.
</objective>
