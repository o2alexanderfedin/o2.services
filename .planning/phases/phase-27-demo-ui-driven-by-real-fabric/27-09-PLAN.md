<objective>
Render the committed benchmark figures — UI-SPEC section 4.7 — and close the packaging
gap UI-SPEC recorded but did not decide: **the footer links `./perf/`, and the built
bundle contains no `perf/` directory.** `docs/perf/build-report.py` writes the HTML into
`docs/perf/`, so the link is correct about where the document lives and wrong about where
the page is served from.

The Benchmarks surface is the one that blocks nothing and reads nothing. It is also the
surface most likely to introduce the defect this phase exists to prevent, and UI-SPEC says
which one: *a number on this surface that is not in that document.* So P9 is the check —
every figure on screen occurs verbatim in `docs/perf/prime-and-pi-benchmarks.md`, asserted
by reading the committed document rather than by trusting the transcription.

Two further requirements from section 4.7, both about not being mistaken for a reading:
the provenance line sits at the top of the surface rather than in a footnote, and every
figure renders in the citation treatment (`--color-neutral-700`) rather than the
Heading-size figure treatment a live reading uses, so a cited figure and a live one are
distinguishable without reading the label.

This is the last surface. `WIRED_SURFACES` reaches all eight here, which turns P1's second
half on for every one of the 91 regions at once.

Purpose: put the measured numbers on screen with their provenance, make the link that
points at them resolve, and finish the enumeration the guard checks against.
Output: `surfaces/bench.ts`, the two cited region groups, the perf report emitted into the
bundle, `WIRED_SURFACES` complete, and `demo-bench.e2e.test.ts` holding P9.
</objective>
