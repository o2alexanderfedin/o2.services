<objective>
Reconcile the ledger with what this phase actually delivered, and record what it did not.

Three things are true at once and the ledger has to carry all three:

1. **The demo now merges through a verified tree-reduce and a person can press the
   button.** `runPi` landed 2026-08-08 with no surface; Plan 27-05 gave it one. The
   MR-03…MR-07 rows say *"The demo still merges with a linear scan: `answerOf` in
   `packages/demo/src/job.ts`, called from `packages/browser/demo/main.ts`"* — that
   sentence is about the **colouring** merge, and the colouring merge is a scan **by
   shape**, not as unwired residue: a colouring is first-found-wins, so there is nothing
   to aggregate, and `runPi`'s own docblock says exactly that about why π is the
   companion and not the replacement. The rows need to distinguish those two facts, which
   they currently do not.
2. **G4's `runJob` half is closed and its primes half is not.** The roadmap entry names
   G4 as *"`runJob` has no caller in the page"*; the v1.1 audit's amended row names the
   open half as the **primes entry point** — five symbols with zero production callers.
   Plan 27-07 closed the first. Plan 27-06 measured the second and left it open with its
   reason. Reporting two workloads through one row is how half of a gap gets recorded as
   all of it, and that is the mistake this row already made once.
3. **Descoped is not satisfied; unmeasured is not met.** Whatever the Primes surface looks
   like on screen, nothing runs on it, and the record has to say so somewhere a reader
   looking for what this phase delivered will actually look.

Purpose: leave the ledger true, and leave the gaps visible with a decider named against
each.
Output: amended MR/DEMO rows, an amended G4 audit row, an updated Phase 27 roadmap entry,
and `27-OPEN-ITEMS.md`.
</objective>
