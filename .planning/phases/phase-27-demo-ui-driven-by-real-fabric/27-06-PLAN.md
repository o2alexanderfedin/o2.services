<objective>
Ship the Primes surface as **Option B — the named absence** — and record it as **open,
not satisfied**.

UI-SPEC section 10 measured why, and the three facts are not inferences:

1. **No signed record vouches for the prime-counting module.**
   `packages/demo/src/kernel-record.ts` exports `KERNEL_RECORD` (colouring) and
   `PI_RECORD`, both signed by `KERNEL_TRUST_ANCHOR`. There is no third record.
   `primesKernelBytes` exists and is checked against `primes.wat`, and nothing vouches
   for it. A tab pins that one anchor, so every executor — including this tab's own —
   refuses a prime-counting dispatch for a provenance failure.
2. **`runJob` cannot carry the input.** Its shards are built as
   `{ value: { a: i }, label: 'public' }`, and the prime kernel reads an eight-byte block
   from `buildPrimesInput(n)`. Even with a record, `runJob` dispatches the wrong bytes.
3. **Re-signing is not free.** `scripts/sign-kernel.ts` mints a new ed25519 key on every
   run and discards the private half, so the existing records cannot be extended. A third
   record means a new anchor, all three records re-signed together, and a change to what
   a stock `o2 agent` and a stock `o2 seed` will run.

**Option A is not planned here and must not be begun here.** It touches the trust root
and it is an owner decision that has been surfaced separately. Do not add
`TabApi.runPrimes`, do not touch `scripts/sign-kernel.ts`, do not regenerate
`kernel-record.ts`.

**And this plan does not close anything.** UI-SPEC says it plainly: *it leaves one of the
two surfaces the roadmap calls load-bearing unable to run anything, and descoped is not
satisfied.* The v1.1 audit is more specific still — **G4's remaining half is the primes
entry point**, five symbols with zero production callers, and Option B calls none of
them. So this plan's job is threefold: render the absence honestly, refuse to add a
control that would produce refusals discovered as timeouts, and **leave behind a
measurement that keeps the gap visible** rather than a sentence claiming it is handled.

Purpose: put the workload on screen as what it is — a module this repository ships, runs
in its own test suite, and cannot dispatch from a tab — without inventing a button, a
figure, or a closure.
Output: `surfaces/primes.ts`, the twelve declared regions at their unavailable copy, no
run control, and `demo-primes.e2e.test.ts` asserting the absence and measuring G4's open
half.
</objective>
