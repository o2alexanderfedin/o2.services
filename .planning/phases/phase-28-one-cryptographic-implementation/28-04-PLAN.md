<objective>
Mint the `CRYPTO-01…06` family and give the certificate-lifecycle facades the ledger row
they have never had.

The facades are built, tested and merged — 28 cases in `cert-lifecycle.test.ts` and 10 in
`cert-lifecycle.browser.test.ts`, and because `cert-lifecycle.test.ts` carries no `.node.`
infix it matches `--project browser` too, so `(28 + 10) × 3` engines `= 114`. The roadmap's
*"28 node tests, 114 browser tests"* is the same 38 cases counted on two tiers, not two
independent suites. They have no requirement id, no verification, no roadmap entry. Under
this repository's own conventions that is the *built, not wired, and not counted* shape
this milestone exists to eliminate — and it was produced by the milestone's own work.

**Placement is load-bearing.** The header arithmetic — `**45 of 72 are [x]**` and its three
companions — is parsed and asserted (`requirements-ledger.node.test.ts:772-776`), and
`V1_BOXES` collects checkboxes **only** under the `## v1 Requirements` heading (`:792-804`),
with the file's own docblock warning that folding other sections in is *"how '82' and '72'
get quoted for the same population"* (`:786-790`). X509 and AOTW both avoided this by
taking their own top-level sections (`.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md:630`, `:649`).
`## Phase 28 Requirements — One Cryptographic Implementation`, inserted after the Phase 26
section's closing `---` and before `## v2 Requirements (deferred)` (`:670`), is the
parsing-safe placement and needs no edit to the header counts. Traceability rows go in the
single table at `:701`+, whose verdict counting is filtered to `V1_BOXES` (`:817-833`), so
extra rows there are safe too — and `**Coverage: 76/76 mapped**` (`:813`) is scoped to the
v1/v1.1 corpus and does not move, exactly as it did not move for X509 or AOTW.

**Six ids, and the status split is not a judgement about effort.** Five rows are `[x]`
**Done** because what they claim is a property of `@o2/core`'s own module graph, its
manifest, or a guard that runs — all reachable. One row, the facades, is `[ ]` **Built, not
wired**, because nothing imports `cert-lifecycle.ts` outside its own two test files. That
is the honest marker and this plan does not improve it by wiring anything: barrel-exporting
the facades is an owner non-decision with a counted cost of 12 callable exports (the
hard-coded ceiling → +12, `OPEN_FINDING_CEILING` → +12), and wiring the port into
`verifyChain`/`verifyCertificate` is a fail-open/fail-closed ruling on a trust path.

**What the phase may claim, one last time.** Neither selection layer was ever in the trust
path — production verification calls `@noble/curves` directly in `capability.ts`'s
`verifyChain`, `enrollment.ts`'s `redeemChallenge`, `enrol` (twice) and `verifyCertificate`,
and `discovery.ts`'s `verifyCapabilityRecord` (re-cited by symbol 2026-08-10; the list read
`capability.ts:219` and the call is at `:249`). The merge is
behaviour-neutral in production. These rows record a duplication removed from a package and
a dependency removed from a tree; none of them may be written as a hazard removed from the
trust path.

Purpose: obligation 3 of six.
Output: six checkbox rows, six traceability rows, a measured before/after parse count, and
one stale triple corrected.
