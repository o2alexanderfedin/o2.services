# Phase 20 · Plan 12 — The second job path stops existing

**Closes:** criterion 1's second clause — *`runResilient` no longer exists as a separate, uncalled
entry point, either merged in or removed* — and WIRE-04's headline: *the fabric has exactly one job
entry point.*

<objective>
`runResilient` has no production caller and never had one. Its machinery now lives inside
`submitJob`, which four submitters actually call. What remains is a **second exported job
implementation**, and WIRE-04's own wording makes that the failure mode rather than an untidiness:
*"Submitting a job gets lease renewal, speculation and coverage accounting **without the caller
choosing between two functions**."* A barrel export is exactly a caller's choice.

**The cost of removal is 32 kernel cases and one adapter, and the risk is not the deletion — it is
the accounting.** `coordinator.test.ts` holds 26 `runResilient` calls and `churn.test.ts` 6. Each
states a behaviour. Some are now covered by 20-01, 20-07, 20-08 and 20-11's kernel cases, some by
their across-process readings, and some may be covered by nothing. **Deleting a suite because its
subject was deleted is how a phase loses a guard and files it as cleanup.**

So this plan's output is a table, case by case, and its deletions follow from the table rather than
preceding it.

**And `remoteDispatch` is the interesting residue.** It exists to preserve the `node`/`task`/`sender`
failure kind that `Executor` deliberately flattens — the very distinction 20-01 chose not to use,
approximating it by counting distinct nodes instead. Whether it goes with `runResilient` or stays
as the record of a distinction the fabric may want back is a decision, and it is written down
either way.
</objective>

- a re-dispatch never re-picks the node that just failed;
- the lease deadline bounds a peer that simply never answers;
- speculation's adaptive threshold, budget, race settlement and late comparison;
- coverage's owed-vs-done gate;
- checkpoint resume, and recovery from an older handle when the newest block is lost;
- `sender`-kind failures are retried like `node` but recorded distinctly.
