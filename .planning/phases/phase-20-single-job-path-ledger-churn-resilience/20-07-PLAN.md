# Phase 20 · Plan 07 — A straggler is duplicated, and the loser is still read

**Closes (mechanism half):** criterion 1's *"speculation … live inside it"* and criterion 3's
mechanism. The live reading and the CLI's cost accounting are 20-09.

<objective>
Every piece of this exists and is unit-verified in Phase 7, and its only caller is `runResilient`,
which nothing calls. `stragglers`, `speculativeCandidates`, `SpeculationLedger` and `settleRace`
have **zero** production callers — measured 2026-08-04.

**Three things `coordinator.ts` got wrong before it got them right, all of which this plan inherits
by construction and none of which its unit tests would catch a second time.** Read that module's
header before writing anything; it names each against its own interest.

1. **Breaking out of the race on the first arrival made `disagreed` impossible on any input.**
   Timing alone picked which of two different CIDs became the answer, and the run reported clean.
   That is majority-vote-by-race, which this project has explicitly refused. The fix: the winner
   returns immediately, and the outstanding copies are compared **after every shard has settled** —
   which costs nothing, because the job was going to wait for its slowest shard anyway. A copy that
   still has not answered by then is reported **uncompared**, not agreeing: you cannot compare an
   answer that never arrived.
2. **A flag that stopped speculating was allowed to stop the timer**, leaving the loop awaiting a
   promise that might never settle. Termination is the lease deadline's job and only its job.
3. **Wrapping each dispatch into its raced form inside the loop** allocated a fresh closure and
   promise per iteration — an out-of-memory crash rather than a slow test, and only when a real
   dispatch is slower than the watchdog, which is exactly when speculation is working.

**And speculation is adaptive rather than a fixed timeout.** A shard is a straggler relative to its
*peers*, so the threshold comes from the median of what has already finished. Early in a job
nothing has finished and nothing is duplicated — correctly, since there is no tail yet. That is why
the loop polls rather than sleeping once for a precomputed deadline.
</objective>

From `packages/core/src/speculation.ts`:

- `DEFAULT_SPECULATION_FRACTION` 0.1, `DEFAULT_STRAGGLER_FACTOR` 1.5, `MIN_SAMPLES` 3.
- `median(values)`, `stragglers(inFlight, now, {completed, factor})` — returns nothing until
  `MIN_SAMPLES` shards have finished, which is why "too new to judge" is the one **non-permanent**
  reason to do nothing.
- `SpeculationLedger({tasks, fraction})` — `request(taskId)`, `duplicated(taskId)`,
  `discard(taskId, nodeId, disagreed)`, `remaining`, `spent`, `multiplier`.
- `speculativeCandidates(request, nodes, exclude)` — **the one eligibility gate**, which is what
  makes CHURN-06 a property of construction rather than a check: a sovereign shard's duplicate can
  only land on its owner's own nodes because the candidate set comes from there.
- `settleRace(answers)` — `RaceOutcome`, with `losers`, `disagreed` and the winner.

From `packages/core/src/coordinator.ts` — `DEFAULT_WATCHDOG_MS` 250, and the `runShard` inner race:
an unconditional `sleep(watchdogMs)` in the `Promise.race`, a `Raced` discriminated union so a tick
cannot be confused with a `null` answer, a `dispatchOn` closure built **once** per generation, and
the `LateOutcome` union whose `'agreed'` arm exists purely so the merge's enumeration is exhaustive
— *"which turns a fifth outcome invented later into a compile error rather than a silent
omission."* Copy the reasoning; do not import the module, which 20-12 deletes.

From `packages/core/src/job/submit.ts` after 20-01 — the generation loop and the re-dispatch
count. Speculation is a **second copy inside one generation**, not a new generation: the lease
holder is unchanged and the winner may be the copy rather than the holder, so the lease is closed
against whoever actually holds it.

From `packages/core/src/job/verify.ts` — `executeVerified` runs its executors with `Promise.all`
and compares them. **Speculation is not redundancy and must not be confused with it**: redundancy
is N executors compared for agreement, speculation is an extra copy of an already-placed shard
because it is slow. A job at redundancy 2 whose straggler is duplicated has three dispatches and
two replicas.

`JobResult` today carries `grossFuel`, `usefulFuel` and `verificationMultiplier`, whose docblock
says the *ratio* is meaningful whatever the unit. The speculation multiplier belongs beside them in
the same shape — `SpeculationLedger.multiplier` is documented as *"Total dispatches over useful
ones, in the shape `verificationMultiplier` uses."*
