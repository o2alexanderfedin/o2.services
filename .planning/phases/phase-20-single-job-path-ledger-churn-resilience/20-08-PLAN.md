# Phase 20 · Plan 08 — The aggregate carries its denominator

**Closes (mechanism half):** criterion 1's *"coverage accounting live inside it"* and criterion 4's
mechanism. The live cross-owner reading and the CLI surface are 20-10.

<objective>
`coverageOf`, `describeCoverage` and `withCoverage` have **zero** production callers — measured
2026-08-04; `runResilient` is the only thing that ever calls one and nothing calls it.
`describeCoverage` already emits criterion 4's literal string, `covered: X/Y owners`.

**Two things will be got wrong here, and both are recorded in the source that is being wired.**

1. **Counting an owner as covered the moment any one of their shards lands overstates coverage
   exactly where it matters most.** `coordinator.ts`'s own comment: *"an owner with four shards,
   three of which failed, would be reported as having contributed, and `complete` would be true
   over a quarter of their data. That is the failure `coverage.ts` exists to prevent, arriving
   through the composition rather than through `coverageOf`."* The gate is **owed vs done, per
   owner**, and it lives in the caller, not in `coverageOf`.

2. **`CoverageReport` alone is the wrong shape for `JobResult`.** `coverageOf` treats an empty
   owner set as not complete — *"An empty job is not a complete one — '0 of 0 owners' answers
   nothing"* — so a public job would render `covered: 0/0 owners — PARTIAL (no owners were
   expected)`, on every benchmark rung in the repository. Carry a **named union**,
   `CoverageReport | 'defines-no-owners'`, the same shape as `'keeps-no-ledger'` and
   `'carries-no-certificate'`. See `20-CONTEXT.md` § *"Coverage: derive the owner set from the
   shards"*.

**And no new `JobSpec` field.** The expected owner set is *derivable from the caller's own input* —
a shard is defined for an owner whether or not that owner's node is up, so an owner with an
unplaceable shard is still expected and correctly lands in `missing`. A required field here would
be a five-site fan-out buying nothing. That is not a contradiction of `onQuorumShortfall`: WIRE-01
is about **choices with silent defaults**, not about values already present in the input.
</objective>

From `packages/core/src/coverage.ts`:

- `CoverageReport` — `covered`, `total`, `missing`, `unexpected`, `complete`.
- `coverageOf(expected, contributed)` — pure set arithmetic. `unexpected` exists because *"silently
  accepting a contribution from an owner outside the job's definition would change the aggregate
  without changing the coverage"*; it is reported, never rejected.
- `describeCoverage(report)` — `covered: X/Y owners — complete`, or `covered: X/Y owners — PARTIAL
  (missing …; unexpected …)`. **Criterion 4's literal string, already written.**
- `withCoverage(value, expected, contributed)` → `CoveredAggregate<T>`, which has *"no `.value`
  shortcut past the report and no unwrap helper, deliberately"*.

From `packages/core/src/coordinator.ts` — the `owedByOwner` / `doneByOwner` pair and the
`filter(([owner, owed]) => (doneByOwner.get(owner) ?? 0) >= owed)` that feeds `coverageOf`. **That
filter is the whole of decision 1 above.** Copy the shape and the comment; do not import the module.

From `packages/core/src/job/submit.ts` — `ShardSpec`'s sovereign arm carries `ownerId: OwnerId`;
the public arm carries none. `JobResult.complete` is *"true only if every shard reached `agreed` at
its full requested redundancy"* — a different question from coverage and it must stay a different
field. A four-owner job in which one owner is entirely absent can have every *placed* shard agree.

From `packages/core/src/job/verify.ts` — a shard "landed" means `verification.status === 'agreed'`.
An `insufficient` or `disagreed` shard did not contribute, and neither did an unplaceable one.
`degraded` is a separate axis: a shard that agreed at reduced redundancy **did** contribute its
owner's data. Decide that explicitly and write the decision down — it is the kind of thing a later
reader will assume was an oversight.

## Recorded decisions

- **No new `JobSpec` field.** The expected owner set is derivable from the caller's own shards.
  WIRE-01 governs choices with silent defaults, not derivable values.
- **An owner contributes only when all of its shards landed.** The alternative reports `complete`
  over a quarter of an owner's data, and `coverage.ts` exists to prevent exactly that.
- **A named union, not a bare `CoverageReport`.** `0/0 owners — PARTIAL` on every public job is what
  the bare shape produces.
- **Coverage and `complete` stay separate questions**, and the pair of independence cases proves it.
- **The `degraded` axis is decided in writing**, either way, because an unstated answer reads as an
  oversight.
- **The reader fan-out is grepped as well as type-checked**, with both counts reported.
