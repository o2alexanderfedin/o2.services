# Phase 20 · Plan 01 — A shard that lost its executor is placed again, under a lease

**Closes (mechanism half):** criterion 1's *"it performs lease renewal … internally"*, criterion
2's re-dispatch mechanism, and the carried Phase 18 criterion 2b clause *"and the requestor
re-picks"*. The **readings** are 20-04 (the armed tripwire) and 20-05 (30 % killed).

<objective>
`submitJob` calls `executeVerified` exactly once per shard. A node that answered the offer while
free and refuses the dispatch, or dies between the two, ends that shard — and nothing tries anyone
else. This plan gives the shard a second generation.

**Two triggers, not one.** `executeVerified` returns `insufficient` only when **every** executor
failed. At redundancy 2 with one executor dead it returns `agreed` with `replicas: 1` — a shard
that silently got less redundancy than it asked for. **Both are re-dispatch triggers.** Treating
only `insufficient` as one is the version of this that looks finished and leaves the more common
case unrepaired.

**The lease is what makes the retry bounded and legible rather than a loop with a counter.**
`LeaseTable` already records `granted`, `renewed`, `completed`, `expired`, `surrendered` and
`abandoned` events, and its own header says why: *"A scheduler that quietly retried would produce
correct answers and inexplicable bills."*

**Renewal has to be built, and it must not become a longer timeout.** `LeaseTable.renew` and
`shouldRenew` have **no caller anywhere** — measured, and the roadmap's `Research: None` line is
wrong about it. Read `20-CONTEXT.md` § *"Lease renewal needs BUILDING, not wiring"* before writing
a line of this. The evidence channel it names already exists on the wire and needs no protocol
change.
</objective>

  generations are spent), `renew`, `complete`, `surrender`, `reap(now, taskId?)`, and the readers
  `holder`, `history`, `redispatches`.
- `shouldRenew(lease, now)` — true at `RENEW_AT` = 2/3 of the span and before expiry.

From `packages/core/src/coordinator.ts` — **the loop to copy the reasoning from, not the code.**
Its `runShard` narrows the node pool by `attempted` **before** placement, with the reason written
out: placement is deterministic rendezvous ranking, so a re-dispatch handed the same pool picks the
node that just failed, every time. Its module header's *"Failure is a fact, silence is a deadline"*
section is the distinction this plan must preserve.

From `packages/core/src/job/verify.ts` — `executeVerified(task, executors)` returns
`insufficient` **only** when every executor failed; otherwise `agreed` with `replicas:
answered.length` and `failures` naming each executor that did not answer.

From `packages/net/src/agent.ts` — the exec branch's capacity slot key is
`` `${request.task.inputCid.toString()}:${request.task.partitionIndex}` `` (search `const slotKey`
in the exec branch). From `packages/core/src/placement.ts` — `LocalCapacity.offer` refuses a second
claim on a held key with `` `${offer.shardId} is already in flight here` `` (search
`is already in flight here`), and `would` is the non-reserving twin.

From `packages/core/src/job/submit.ts` as it stands: `JobSpec.admit?: AdmissionControl` selects
`planWithOffers` over `planPlacement`, and its own doc records that the two *"are alternatives and
are never composed"* — feeding one into the other removes the re-pick. **The generation loop must
not violate that**: each generation runs one placer over a *narrowed candidate list*, never one
placer over the other's output.
