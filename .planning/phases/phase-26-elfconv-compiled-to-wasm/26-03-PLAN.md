decision is not the planner's and not the executor's. `26-CONTEXT.md`: *"No wave beyond

before the measurement is in.** The precedent is already carried in this repository:
AOT-05 is *"a measured negative, reported unmet rather than reworded"*
(`REQUIREMENTS.md:626` — *"It was measured with two controls and the answer is no."*).

`longjmp` or any other symbol preview1 does not have, then Phase 26's output is that
name and the evidence for it, and that is a complete phase rather than a failed one.
What would be a failure is widening what counts as passing —
CLAUDE.md: *"Descoped is not satisfied; unmeasured is not met."*

Purpose: close the loop honestly, in both directions, and gate the next spend on a
decision the owner makes rather than one a plan assumed.
