## Phase Goal

**As a** browser visitor arriving from a Telegram message, **I want to** land somewhere that
works, with the copy that brought me there promising exactly what the licensing ruling will
still support a year later, **so that** what I was promised is what I actually get — with the
contribution posture written down before the first outside pull request arrives.

<objective>
The half of this phase that no agent can finish.

Criterion 1's own words: *"verified by opening the **real** recruitment link from a **real**
Telegram message on **both iOS and Android**. A green obtained from a spoofed user-agent string
does not satisfy this: the point of the check is the engine, not the string."* Criterion 2's:
*"recorded from those two devices … a phase that reports the general pattern without opening
the link has reported the thing that was already known."*

Neither is an engineering judgement and neither has an emulator answer. So this plan writes the
script, pauses for the owner, and then transcribes what came back — and it moves the ledger on
the readings rather than on the plan.

Purpose: `38-DEVICE-OBSERVATIONS.md` already records three devices, both browsers, a working
page and an automatic reconnect on wake. It also names, in its own words, exactly what is still
missing: whether the engine was **suspended** or the socket merely **dropped**; **backgrounding
while the device stays awake**, which is *"the state a volunteer is in most of the time"*; and
IndexedDB across the transition. Those three are what this run collects.

Output: an owner script with a no-go reading per step, a recording table, and a ledger moved by
exactly as much as the readings support.
</objective>
