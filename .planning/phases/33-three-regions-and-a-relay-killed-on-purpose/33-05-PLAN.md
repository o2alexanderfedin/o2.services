## The reading is comparative inside one run, never against a threshold

`CLAUDE.md` § Measurement: an absolute threshold silently encodes the machine, the load and the
I/O weather of the day it was written. Phase 32's idle-versus-busy window
(`.planning/ROADMAP.md:2383-2390`) is the worked example — the busy window moved the relay
**less** than the idle one, and an absolute *flat* would have failed a fabric doing exactly the
right thing. So the drill takes the same synthetic population through the same arrangement twice,
once with all three answering and once with one killed, and reports the delta between the two
arms.
