## Two rules, because the criterion names two failures

Criterion 2 is at `.planning/ROADMAP.md:2425` and is **cited rather than quoted here**: its own
text contains an illustrative place name, and a plan that quoted it would fail the guard it is
commissioning. Read it there.

It names two distinguishable failures, and the guard answers each with its own rule:

- **Tier 1 — a term, anywhere on the published surface.** A place name in published copy or in a
  results document is a violation regardless of what sentence it sits in.
- **Tier 2 — an attribution, anywhere in the tree.** A line naming one of the three region
  addresses together with a placement verb is a violation even when the place it names is not on
  any list. This is the half that catches an invented place, and it is what makes Tier 1's list
  a floor rather than a ceiling.

## What this guard must NOT fire on, stated before it is written

The connectivity funnel carries a **two-letter country code about a visitor**
(`packages/cloudflare/src/funnel-collector.ts:9-134`, which already argues at length that a
country is not a person and that the code is never derived inside the Worker). That is where a
**visitor** is. `HOST-07` is about where a hosted **object** runs. The two are different claims
and the guard must not confuse them: Tier 1 holds place **names**, never two-letter codes, and
Tier 2 fires only on a line that also names a region address.
