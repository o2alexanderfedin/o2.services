`SeedServer` (`packages/node/src/seed-server.ts`) serves the demo page itself — `ROOT` with
`PAGE_PATH = '/packages/browser/demo/index.html'` — and `/bootstrap.json` from the same origin.
**So a tab loaded from the seed's own HTTP port exercises the production discovery path**, not
a re-creation of it. `seed.httpPort`, `seed.wsPort`, `seed.node`, `seed.joinUrl` are public.
After 24-06 `SeedServerOptions.relayAdmission` is required.

`TabApi` (`packages/browser/src/tab-api.ts`), reachable as `window.o2` from
`packages/browser/demo/main.ts`:

  - `start(options)` / `stop()`
  - `dial(address: string): Promise<string>` — **this is what makes the limit assertable at the
    browser tier**: handed a direct address, the tab reaches a peer it could not find.
  - `connectDiscoveredPeers(): Promise<TabDiscoveryRound>` where `TabDiscoveryRound` is
    `{ asked, dialed, failed, upgrades, relayedOnly, stalled }`. `dialed` holds **peer ids**;
    `failed` holds **addresses**.
  - `discoverRelays(): Promise<{ source: 'query' | 'origin' | 'none'; relayAddrs: string[] }>`
  - `peers(): string[]`

`demo/main.ts`'s `runDiscoveryRound` builds candidates in two steps, and **both are gated by
the same reservation stores**: step 1 fetches `/bootstrap.json` and pushes every string in
`info.peerAddrs`; step 2 calls `findReservedPeers` over the fabric, which reads the same
`reservations` thunk. So an uncertificated peer that reserved nowhere is absent from both.

`browser-node.ts` passes `reservations: 'relays-for-nobody'`, so **a tab answers `[]` about
itself whatever its admission state**. That is a tautology and it must be demonstrated in both
arms rather than warned about — `gated-admission.e2e.test.ts` establishes the idiom.

The two-step provider start (`gated-admission.e2e.test.ts:223`): a provider's issuer key does
not exist until `issuesCertificates` has been through a start, and a provider restarting on the
**same** `blockstoreDir` keeps it across a different pid. The minting start states
`relayAdmission: new Set<PublicKeyHex>()` — admits nobody — because it meets nobody and stating
the open literal would put another open site in the repository-wide census.

`gated-admission.e2e.test.ts` runs chromium + firefox + webkit at `real 24.96` for 4 cases. It
is the budget precedent.
