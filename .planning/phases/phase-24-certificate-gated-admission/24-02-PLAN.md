# Phase 24 · Plan 02 — The rigs and the demo say what they are, before anything is armed

**Closes (mechanism, second third):** the precondition that stops criterion **8** from silently
reshaping a published measurement. Nothing here refuses anybody either.

> **NUMBERING CORRECTED 2026-08-06.** This said *"criterion 1"*, as every plan in this phase did.
> `ROADMAP.md`'s `### Phase 24: Certificate-Gated Admission` block numbers it **8** — the number it
> kept when the owner moved it out of Phase 19 on 2026-08-04. **Phase 24 has exactly one criterion**,
> so a future verification scores it *out of 1, not out of 8*; the digit is an inherited label, not
> a position in a list.

<objective>
`bin/bench.ts` stands up *"every node this driver has ever built"* with no certificate, and it
publishes a scaling curve. `demo/main.ts` leaves tabs unenrolled unless a user key is supplied — and
**already renders `'carries-no-certificate'` as a first-class state**, which is an asset this plan
builds on rather than a gap it closes.

> **CORRECTION, 2026-08-05, retained for the reasoning.** This paragraph previously read
> *"`bin/bench.ts` stands up … with no certificate, and `perf-workload.ts` does the same. One of
> them publishes a scaling curve."* The pairing was wrong, and it was wrong in a way an executor
> would have burned an hour on: `packages/bench/src/perf-workload.ts` holds **zero**
> `relayAdmission` occurrences and constructs **no `FabricNode`** — it composes `serveAgent`
> directly, passing `reservations: 'relays-for-nobody'` at both of its two sites. A rig with no
> relay has no reservation to gate and therefore **no admission posture to state**. It is out of
> scope for this plan and has been removed from `files_modified`. The reasoning that put it here
> was "both files are in-process rigs that never enrol", which is true and is not the same thing
> as "both files build relay-capable nodes".

If 24-03 arms the gate first, every one of those numbers moves and **nobody can attribute the
movement**. Was it admission? Was it the extra handshake? Was it the rig quietly falling back? A
published measurement whose conditions changed underneath it is worse than no measurement, and this
repository has the standing rule that says so.

So this plan does two things and neither of them is a refusal: it makes every rig and demo surface
**state** its posture at its own call site, and it **records the pre-gate baseline** that 24-04 will
compare against.
</objective>
