`single-issuer-quorum` refusal. Its plant follows M40's existing idiom in the ledger: replace
the condition with `false as boolean`, which compiles, changes nothing else, and restores
exactly the pre-phase behaviour of composing a one-issuer member set.

**Decision (b) — the issuer conjunct in `classifyAttestation`.** The expression
`if (operators.size >= 2 && issuers.size >= 2) return 'independent'`. Its plant drops the
conjunct, i.e. becomes `if (operators.size >= 2) return 'independent'` — literally the line the
phase replaced, so a green here would mean the phase's central claim is unguarded.

**What must NOT be planted, because it is inert.** Relaxing the **first** branch's operator
threshold (`operators.size >= 1 && issuers.size >= 2`) is unreachable on a one-issuer fixture:
`issuers.size >= 2` is false, control falls through, and the answer is unchanged. That is the
Phase 44 shape exactly — a mutation downstream of a decision that has already been taken — and
it is why M42's plant was re-sited in 45-01 onto the branch that actually decides for the
fixtures that catch it.

Ledger shape (`packages/node/src/mutation-ledger.ts`): each entry is
`{ id, why, file, find, replace, caughtBy, signature, signatureSource }`. `signatureSource` is
`'test-title'` (checked by the cheap layer against the catching file's text) or

unique; `why` must exceed 40 characters; `find` must occur **exactly once** in `file`.
