the preference) on the live job path per the owner's ruling, and leave every guard and every
case in `packages/core/src/quorum.test.ts` green.

becomes `single-issuer`, and the day a second provider runs `independent` becomes reachable
with no code change.

cases that carry criteria 1, 2 and the ordering half of 3.
