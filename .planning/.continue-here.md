## 🔴 THE OWNER RULING THAT REMOVED WORK

`.planning/consults/2026-08-14-owner-ruling-sovereign-threat-model.md` — **read it before
designing anything on the sovereign path.**

> Sovereign data is processed at rest, the requestor IS the data owner, so there is no interest in
> faking a result about one's own data; and a bad storage node has a blast radius of one owner's
> own data.

It killed two designs drafted the same day. MR-05's rendezvous check defended against *"a malicious
requestor concentrating every combine on one node"* — **that is the data owner attacking their own
job**. MR-04's heavy option (publish the contribution set on a frame) went with it.

**The boundary, so it is not lost:** `PROJECT.md:31` keeps **cross-owner** aggregation verified,
and the public path keeps N-version redundancy plus commit-reveal. Neither is relaxed.

**The general rule:** design against the threat model you have, not the one the literature assumes.
Both drafts came from a source reasoning about open adversarial marketplaces.
