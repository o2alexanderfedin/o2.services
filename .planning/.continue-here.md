**A reading that would be identical if the mechanism never ran is not evidence of the
mechanism.** This appeared **three times in one session**, in three different places, and
twice it had been standing for months as if it were proof.

| Where | The reading | Why it evidenced nothing |

A page implementing the wrong one passed |
| **VER-03** (not done) | the fabric surface's `· shared relay: <peer id>` | derived by `attestationReceipt` from **certificates, not the composer**. Identical if `composeQuorum` never ran. **This one is still live — do not close VER-03 on it** |

**The test:** ask what the output would be if the mechanism were deleted. If the answer is
"the same", the reading is about something else.
